## Behavior and breakpoints

No Breakpoint property: one size at every width.

- **Circle:** 16x16px, fully round. Unselected: `color/gray/max` fill, 1px inside `color/gray/200` border (6.6:1 on white).
- **Touch area:** 40x40px, 12px (`spacing/150`) around the circle, 5px corners (`radius/utility`). With a label the control stays 40px tall: the label starts 8px (`spacing/100`) after the circle, inside the touch area, with 8px (`spacing/100`) above and below.
- **Label:** Noto Sans SemiBold 18px (`font/size/18`, `font/style/semibold`), 24px line height, `color/gray/100` (11.6:1 on white), 8px (`spacing/100`) from the circle (production 10px; Karl, 2026-11-12).
- **Selected:** `color/theme/primary` circle with an 8px `color/gray/max` dot (like CheckBox's selected box; Karl, 2026-11-12); hover and pressed `color/theme/primary-lighter`. Review sites: dot 4.8–9.3:1 on primary, 3.1–5.8:1 on primary-lighter.
- **Unselected hover / pressed:** the border turns `color/gray/min`.
- **Disabled:** `color/gray/600` circle; unselected keeps a 1px `color/gray/500` border, selected shows a `color/gray/500` dot; label `color/gray/300` (disabled text is exempt from contrast).
- **Focus (inFocus):** a 2px `color/gray/black` ring outside the 40x40 touch area around the circle, not the label, 5px corners; it overlaps the start of the label, which is fine (Karl, 2026-11-12). Any status; hover and pressed have no ring.
- **Pointer:** changes from an arrow to a pointing hand on the circle's touch area or the label.

## Accessibility

- No InFocus state in Figma.
- Text color on its background must meet WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 for text under 18px) in every theme; theme colors change per site.
- Icons inside a labelled control are decorative (`aria-hidden`); an icon-only control needs an accessible name (`aria-label`).
- A real `input type="radio"` for each choice, grouped by `name` inside a `fieldset` with a `legend`; the label is a `label` tied to its input.
- Keyboard: Tab moves into and out of the group (to the selected choice, or the first); arrow keys move the choice within the group; Space selects the focused choice.
- Focus is the 2px `color/gray/black` ring around the circle's 40x40 touch area (WCAG 2.4.7); the circle's border is 6.6:1 (WCAG 1.4.11).
